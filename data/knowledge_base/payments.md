# Payments

## Accepted payment methods
We accept UPI, credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, RuPay, Amex), net banking, Acme Wallet and cash on delivery (COD).

## Cash on delivery
COD is available for orders up to ₹10,000. A ₹25 handling fee applies to COD orders. Please keep exact change ready; couriers can also accept UPI at the doorstep.

## Failed payments
If money was debited but the order failed, the amount is automatically reversed by your bank within 5-7 business days. You do not need to raise a request.

## EMI
No-cost EMI is available on orders above ₹3,000 with select credit cards for 3 and 6 month tenures.
