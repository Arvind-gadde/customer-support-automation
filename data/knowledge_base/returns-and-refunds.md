# Returns and Refunds

## Return window
Most items can be returned within 10 days of delivery. Electronics have a 7-day replacement-only window for manufacturing defects. Innerwear, personal care products and gift cards are non-returnable.

## Condition of returned items
Items must be unused, with original tags and packaging. Electronics must include all accessories, manuals and the original box.

## How refunds are paid
Refunds go back to the original payment method. UPI and card refunds take 5-7 business days after the return passes quality check. Cash-on-delivery (COD) orders are refunded to your Acme Wallet instantly, or to a bank account of your choice within 7 business days.

## Partial refunds
If only some items in an order are returned, only those items are refunded. Shipping fees are refunded only when the return is due to our error (wrong, damaged or defective item).

## Refund approval
Small refunds are processed automatically. Larger refunds are reviewed by our support team, usually within 4 business hours.
