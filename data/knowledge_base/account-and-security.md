# Account and Security

## Changing your phone number or email
Go to Profile > Account Settings. A one-time password (OTP) is sent to both the old and new contact to confirm the change.

## Suspicious activity
We will never ask for your OTP, password, or full card number over chat, phone or email. If you notice an order you did not place, contact support immediately so we can secure your account.

## Deleting your account
You can request account deletion from Profile > Privacy. Deletion completes within 30 days. Orders in transit must be delivered or cancelled first.
