# Shipping and Delivery

## Delivery timelines
Standard delivery takes 3-5 business days in metro cities and 5-8 business days elsewhere in India. Express delivery (available in 40+ metro cities) arrives in 1-2 business days. Orders placed after 2 PM IST are dispatched the next business day.

## Shipping fees
Standard delivery is free on orders of ₹499 or more. Orders below ₹499 carry a ₹49 shipping fee. Express delivery costs ₹99 regardless of order value.

## Tracking your order
A tracking number is emailed and sent by SMS once the order ships. Tracking usually updates within 24 hours of dispatch. You can also ask this assistant for the status of any order on your account.

## Failed delivery attempts
The courier attempts delivery up to three times. After three failed attempts the package is returned to our warehouse and a full refund is issued to the original payment method within 7 business days.
